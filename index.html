Sargam notation

S r R g G m M P d D n N — capitals shuddh, lowercase komal (re ga dha ni); m = shuddh Ma, M = teevra Ma · dot above / X' = taar saptak, dot below / .X = mandra saptak · – ≈ 0.3 s held
ornaments: (R)G = kan (grace) · (GRG)m = murki · X~Y = meend (glide) · ≈X = andolan/gamak (oscillation) · ~X = glide within the note

Clean keeps only confident, singable notes — drops blips and stray pitches, snaps rare off-scale notes onto the song's scale, and groups the song into verse lines. Detailed adds kan, murki, meend and andolan for alaap work. Raw shows everything the tracker heard. Raise strictness if background instruments still leak in; lower it if quiet phrases go missing.

Audio is processed entirely on this device — nothing is uploaded, no analytics, no third-party assets. Pitch transposes the recording to your own Sa (notation is unchanged — sargam is the same in any key); Speed slows it down without changing pitch.
