How to read & use the contour ▾

Click to seek · drag to pan · ⌘/Ctrl+scroll or ± to zoom · drag along the ruler at the top to loop a passage (double-click it to clear). The line is the melody: indigo plateaus are notes to hold, while saffron connectors use three shapes: a vertical step, a compact hook for a quick bend, or a broad curve for meend. Each dot is a note the voice hits, named with its swara; solid grid lines are the song’s scale. With Sing along on, your voice draws as a teal trail over the melody.

Every sung syllable starts a fresh note (so repeated words on one pitch don't blur into one). Clean keeps the singable notes and the ornaments that touch them — murkis as (GRG)m, bends as P~S' — while dropping blips and stray pitches. Detailed shows every nuance; Simple is just the main notes. Raise strictness if background instruments leak in; lower it if quiet phrases go missing.

Audio is processed entirely on this device — nothing is uploaded, no analytics, no third-party assets. Sing-along uses your microphone live and in memory only — nothing is ever recorded or transmitted. Pitch transposes the recording to your own Sa (notation is unchanged — sargam is the same in any key); Speed slows it down without changing pitch.
