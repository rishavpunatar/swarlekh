Sargam notation
How to read the sargam ▾

S r R g G m M P d D n N — capitals shuddh, lowercase komal (re ga dha ni); m = shuddh Ma, M = teevra Ma · dot above / X' = taar saptak, dot below / .X = mandra saptak · – ≈ 0.3 s held
ornaments: (R)G = kan (grace) · (GRG)m = murki · S⌒R⌒G = meend gliding through those swaras · ≈g(R–g) = andolan oscillating between those swaras · ~X = glide within the note

Every sung syllable starts a fresh note (so repeated words on one pitch don't blur into one). Clean keeps the singable notes and the ornaments that touch them — murkis as (GRG)m, bends as P~S' — while dropping blips and stray pitches. Detailed shows every nuance; Simple is just the main notes. Raise strictness if background instruments leak in; lower it if quiet phrases go missing.

Audio is processed entirely on this device — nothing is uploaded, no analytics, no third-party assets. Pitch transposes the recording to your own Sa (notation is unchanged — sargam is the same in any key); Speed slows it down without changing pitch.
